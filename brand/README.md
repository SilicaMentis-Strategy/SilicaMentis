# SilicaMentis brand files

Files that belong to the brand but not to the website.

## Folders
- `instagram/` – profile images for @silicamentis (Flint and Paper versions).
- `teams-backgrounds/` – Microsoft Teams call backgrounds, dark and light, 1920×1080.

## Logos
The master logo files are in `website/assets/` (favicon, mark, reversed mark, watermark, email logo, social share image). They are not copied here, so there is only one version of each.

## Palette and type
Flint #2A2D31, Flint deep #1F2124, Paper #F2EEE6, Paper deep #E6DFD1, Spark #C6531B, Spark dark #A9441A, Steel #555A5F, Steel light #B9BDC0, Rule #CFC7B8.
IBM Plex Serif (headings, wordmark), IBM Plex Sans (body), IBM Plex Mono ("STRATEGY" sub-mark, letter-spaced).
