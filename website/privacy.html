01Who we are

SilicaMentis Strategy is a trading name of Kieran Hanrahan, based in Ireland. For the purposes of data protection law, Kieran Hanrahan is the controller of the personal data described here.

For any question about this notice or your data, email kieran@silicamentis.eu.

02What we collect

Only what you choose to give us, plus the minimum technical data needed to run the website:

  • Enquiry form: your answers to the four questions (role, goals, what you have tried, how we can help), your name, work email, organisation and any note you add.
  • Correspondence: emails and notes from any conversation that follows.
  • Technical data: the web host keeps standard server logs (such as IP address, browser type and pages requested) for security and to keep the site running.

We do not ask for, and ask you not to send, special category data such as health information.

03Why we use it, and on what basis

PurposeLawful basis (GDPR)

Your details are not added to a mailing list, sold, or used for profiling or automated decisions that affect you.

04Who it is shared with

Only with service providers that process data on our behalf, under written terms that require them to protect it:

  • Form service: Tally (Tally BV, Belgium). Form answers are stored in the EU, on Google Cloud in Belgium. Tally's email notifications are sent through SendGrid, a US provider.
  • Email provider: Microsoft 365, supplied through GoDaddy
  • Website host: Netlify (Netlify, Inc., United States)
  • Typefaces: Google Fonts (Google Ireland Limited and Google LLC), which receives your IP address when a page loads – see section 08.

We do not share your data with anyone else unless the law requires it.

05Transfers outside the EEA

Where a provider processes data outside the European Economic Area – for example, the email service Tally uses to send form notifications – the transfer relies on an EU adequacy decision (including, for certified US providers, the EU–US Data Privacy Framework) or on the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses. EU-based providers are preferred where one is available.

06How long it is kept

  • Enquiries that do not lead to an engagement: deleted within 12 months of the last contact.
  • Client records: kept for the duration of the engagement and then for six years, to meet tax and accounting obligations.
  • Server logs: kept for the period set by the web host, Netlify, and then deleted.

07Your rights

You have the right to:

  • access the personal data we hold about you
  • have it corrected if it is wrong
  • have it erased
  • restrict or object to how it is used
  • receive it in a portable format
  • withdraw consent at any time, where consent is relied on

Email kieran@silicamentis.eu to use any of these rights. We will reply within one month.

If you are unhappy with how your data has been handled, you can complain to the Data Protection Commission, 6 Pembroke Row, Dublin 2, D02 X963, through its online contact form.

08Cookies

This website does not set any cookies, and it does not use analytics, advertising or tracking tools.

The site's typefaces are loaded from Google Fonts. When a page loads, your browser connects to Google's servers, which receive your IP address. Google states that Google Fonts does not set cookies.

If analytics or any other cookies are added in future, this notice will be updated first, and your consent will be asked for before any non-essential cookie is set.

09Use of AI tools

Prototyping work uses AI models from third-party providers. Where your information is processed by an AI tool:

  • only providers whose business terms do not use your inputs to train their models are used
  • providers processing data in the EU are preferred where available
  • the providers involved in an engagement are named in its written scope before any of your data is used

10Mutual confidentiality

Strategic questions are sensitive. From the first conversation onwards, both parties agree to the following commitments on confidential information.

What counts as confidential

Any non-public information either party shares in connection with an enquiry or engagement, whether spoken, written or in data. This includes business plans, strategy, financial information, customer and market data, and the prototypes, methods and pricing of SilicaMentis Strategy.

What SilicaMentis Strategy commits to

  • to use your confidential information only to assess and deliver the work you have asked for
  • not to disclose it to anyone, except service providers who need it for that work and are bound by confidentiality
  • to keep it in a private, sandboxed environment and never place it on a live system without your explicit written consent
  • not to enter it into any AI tool whose terms allow inputs to be used for model training
  • to return or delete it on request, or at the end of an engagement, except where the law requires a record to be kept

What you commit to

  • to keep confidential any non-public methods, prototypes and pricing that SilicaMentis Strategy shares with you, and to use them only to assess or receive the work

Exceptions

These commitments do not cover information that is already public through no fault of the receiving party, was already known to them, is received lawfully from someone else, or is developed independently. Either party may disclose information where the law or a regulator requires it, giving the other notice where permitted.

How long it lasts

These commitments last for five years from the last exchange of information, and for trade secrets for as long as they remain trade secrets. They are governed by the law of Ireland.

If you would prefer a signed mutual non-disclosure agreement before the first conversation, ask for one at kieran@silicamentis.eu. Any paid engagement is covered by its own written terms, which include these commitments.

11Changes to this notice

This notice may be updated as the website or services change. The date at the top shows when it was last revised.
